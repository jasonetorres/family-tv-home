# Space fact pack. One fact per line. No trademarked fiction.
The Sun is a star.
Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System.
Saturn's rings are made mostly of ice.
Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos.
A light-year is how far light travels in one year.
The Moon is about 384,000 kilometers from Earth on average.
Neptune was predicted with math before it was seen.
Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.
A day on the Moon lasts about an Earth month.
The Milky Way is the galaxy that holds our Solar System.
Venus is the hottest planet in the Solar System.
Astronauts float in orbit because they are falling around Earth.
The International Space Station circles Earth about every 90 minutes.
Uranus rotates on its side compared with the other planets.
There are more stars in the sky than grains of sand on Earth's beaches is a common estimate, and the real count is enormous.
Comets grow a tail when they warm up near the Sun.
Pluto is a dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt.
Earth's magnetic field helps shield us from solar wind.
A solar eclipse happens when the Moon blocks the Sun.
The James Webb Space Telescope looks at infrared light.
